# Design Audit Judge Review

## Context
You are reviewing an initial design audit (Step 1 output) to ensure accuracy, proper prioritization, and actionability.

## Your Task (Step 2: Judge)

Review the Step 1 audit and:

1. **Validate Findings**: Are the identified issues real? Are severity ratings appropriate?
2. **Check Specificity**: Are recommendations specific enough to implement?
3. **Prioritize**: Which findings have the highest impact for effort?
4. **Answer Questions**: Address any clarifying questions from Step 1
5. **Add Missing Issues**: Did the audit miss anything obvious?
6. **Remove False Positives**: Flag any findings that are incorrect or too nitpicky
