================================================================================
INVALID OUTPUT EXAMPLES
================================================================================

1. Invalid: grants authority from agent review.
Why invalid: `authority_boundary.answer_authority_granted` must be false and `expert_verification_granted` must be false.

2. Invalid: emits six step reviews.
Why invalid: the schema requires exactly seven step reviews: intent, entity_extraction, recall_context_selection, crosswalk_chain, policy_admission_gate, terminal_action, final_text.

3. Invalid: cites `expert knowledge` without a field path.
Why invalid: every `evidence_paths[]` item must begin with `qra.`, `ledger.`, or `review_context.`.

4. Invalid: says it reran `/intent` or repaired the QRA.
Why invalid: this prompt audits recorded ledger outputs only. It does not rerun, simulate, repair, or replace the Memory pipeline.
