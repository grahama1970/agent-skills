You are a cybersecurity requirements analyst. Given a Question-Response-Assertion (QRA) pair from the SPARTA aerospace security framework, generate variations that lead to the EXACT SAME answer.

INPUT:
- question: {question}
- answer: {answer}
- control_id: {control_id}

GENERATE exactly 6 variations. Each must lead to the same answer verbatim.

## Expertise-Level Variations (3)

Rephrase the question at three expertise levels. The answer stays identical.

1. LAYPERSON: Plain language, no jargon.
2. PROJECT_MANAGER: Business/program language. Risk, schedule, compliance impact.
3. CYBERSECURITY_EXPERT: Technical depth. Framework terminology, specific standards.

## Reversal-Curse Variations (3)

Flip the question direction so it approaches from the answer side.

1. REVERSAL_CAUSE: Given the answer as a premise, ask what would cause or require it.
2. REVERSAL_DETECTION: Given the answer as a premise, ask how you would detect or verify it.
3. REVERSAL_ABSENCE: Ask what would happen if the answer's content were NOT in place.

## GOOD EXAMPLE

Input:
- question: "What is a key mitigation measure against Active Scanning (RF/Optical)?"
- answer: "Implementing frequency hopping and signal encryption reduces the effectiveness of active scanning by making signals harder to intercept and analyze."
- control_id: "ESA-T2001"

Output:
```json
{
  "variations": [
    {"level": "LAYPERSON", "type": "expertise", "question": "How can a satellite protect itself from someone trying to listen in on its radio signals?"},
    {"level": "PROJECT_MANAGER", "type": "expertise", "question": "What countermeasure should the program implement to reduce risk from adversary RF scanning of our spacecraft communications?"},
    {"level": "CYBERSECURITY_EXPERT", "type": "expertise", "question": "Per ESA-T2001, what COMSEC technique mitigates active RF reconnaissance against spacecraft telemetry links?"},
    {"level": "REVERSAL_CAUSE", "type": "reversal", "question": "Why would a spacecraft program implement frequency hopping and signal encryption on its communication links?"},
    {"level": "REVERSAL_DETECTION", "type": "reversal", "question": "How would you verify that frequency hopping and signal encryption are properly deployed on a spacecraft's RF links?"},
    {"level": "REVERSAL_ABSENCE", "type": "reversal", "question": "What vulnerability exists if a spacecraft does NOT use frequency hopping or signal encryption on its communication channels?"}
  ]
}
```

## BAD EXAMPLE (DO NOT DO THIS)

- BAD: Adding new information not in the answer: "Implementing frequency hopping, signal encryption, AND directional antennas..."
- BAD: Changing the answer scope: "What are ALL mitigation measures..." (original asks for "a key" measure)
- BAD: Vague layperson: "How do you protect stuff?" (too generic, doesn't constrain to the same answer)
- BAD: Reversal that changes the answer: "What happens if there's no encryption?" (answer is about both frequency hopping AND encryption together)
- BAD: Expert question that narrows too much: "What specific FHSS algorithm..." (answer doesn't specify algorithm)
