# Example: Multi-Asset Kling Prompt

Assets:

```text
@Maya_Ren_v01
@Crystal_Key_v01
@Neon_Alley_v01
```

Prompt:

```text
Shot 1 (3s): [@Maya_Ren_v01] walks into [@Neon_Alley_v01] from the foreground, holding [@Crystal_Key_v01] low in her right hand.
Camera: medium tracking shot from behind, slight handheld movement.
Background: rain falls, steam rises, red and blue neon reflections ripple in puddles.

Shot 2 (3s): [@Maya_Ren_v01] raises [@Crystal_Key_v01] and the blue crystal begins to pulse.
Camera: close-up on the key and her gloved hand, slow push-in.
Background: blue light reflects across the wet pavement and vending machine.

Shot 3 (4s): [@Maya_Ren_v01] looks over her shoulder, alert, as the alley lights flicker.
Camera: medium close-up, shallow depth of field, camera slowly circles toward her front.
Background: rain continues, steam curls through neon light.

Keep consistent across all shots:
Maya has short black curly hair, green eyes, scar above left eyebrow, cream bomber jacket, red fingerless gloves, silver pendant. The key is dark iron, palm-sized, with a circular bow, cracked blue crystal, three jagged teeth, and star engraving. The alley has a red noodle sign on the left, blue vending machine on the right, wet pavement, overhead cables, and stairs at the far end.

Avoid:
duplicated Maya, changing clothes, losing the scar, changing the key shape, changing the crystal color, moving the noodle sign to the wrong side, turning the alley into daytime, distorted hands.
```
