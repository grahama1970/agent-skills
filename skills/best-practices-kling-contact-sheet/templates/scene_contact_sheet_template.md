# Scene / Environment Contact Sheet Template

Scenes need layout, landmarks, materials, lighting, and allowed ambient motion. Do not treat them like character turnarounds.

## Required references

```text
01_wide_establishing_view.png
Purpose: Overall identity, composition, layout, scale.
Requirements: includes key landmarks and enough floor/sky/entrance context.

02_alternate_camera_angle.png
Purpose: Shows spatial depth and where objects are in relation to each other.
Requirements: same place, same landmarks, no contradictory layout.

03_key_landmark_detail.png
Purpose: Locks important sign, altar, door, statue, counter, cockpit, mountain, tree, etc.
Requirements: clear crop and material details.

04_lighting_mood_reference.png
Purpose: Locks time of day, weather, atmosphere, color temperature.
Requirements: do not mix day/night unless making variants.
```
