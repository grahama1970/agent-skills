# Effect Template

Use this for magic effects, particles, fire, smoke, fog, dust, weather bursts, sparks, force fields, glows, and other reusable visual effects.

## Required references

```text
01_main_effect_shape.png
Purpose: Overall recognizable shape and color.

02_particle_or_texture_closeup.png
Purpose: Density, opacity, granularity, edges, texture.

03_scale_or_interaction.png
Purpose: Relationship to character, prop, ground, wall, or scene.

04_motion_phase_or_lighting.png
Purpose: Direction, speed impression, emission, shadows, lighting interaction.
```
