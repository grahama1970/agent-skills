# Prop / Item Contact Sheet Template

Use this for tools, devices, products, weapons-as-fictional-props, accessories, handheld items, furniture, vehicles-as-set-pieces, and hero objects.

## Required references

```text
01_main_hero_angle.png
Purpose: Most recognizable shape and personality.
Requirements: uncluttered, clear silhouette, material visible.

02_side_top_or_back.png
Purpose: Locks geometry and construction.
Requirements: different angle, not a redundant duplicate.

03_scale_or_in_hand.png
Purpose: Shows size relative to hand/body/table/door.
Requirements: clear scale cue; specify holder/background as ignore if temporary.

04_material_or_marking_detail.png
Purpose: Locks logo, engraving, buttons, damage, seam, texture, screen, crystal, etc.
Requirements: sharp detail and consistent design.
```
