# Speaker-note guidance

The authoritative notes live in the deck YAML. Preserve these delivery rules when editing
in Google Slides:

- Say “late-stage prototype” and “prepared-host” rather than implying turnkey deployment.
- Say “bounded dated receipt” before quoting any count or pass.
- Say “synthetic F-36 program world” on first mention.
- Repeat that candidate visibility is not compliance credit.
- Describe degraded or blocked states as truthful product behavior.
- Do not say that every response uses the governed route while transitional paths remain.
- End with one representative-claim walkthrough as the collaboration ask.
