# Sparta Explorer deck brief

## Audience

Aerospace and defense design partners, assessors, cyber analysts, engineers, program
leads, and integration teams evaluating an evidence-governed assurance workbench.

## Deck job

Earn a bounded next conversation: trace one representative space-cyber claim through
framework source, program relationship, component or supplier lineage, evidence,
uncertainty, review state, and final human decision.

## Position

Sparta Explorer is not “ChatGPT for compliance.” It is a local-first investigation
workspace where the model helps navigate, evidence carries support, and authorized people
retain decision authority.

## Narrative

```text
fragmented evidence and authority
  -> three immutable boundaries
  -> one inspectable decision thread
  -> synthetic F-36 demonstration
  -> real prepared-host product surfaces
  -> bounded proof and honest gaps
  -> design-partner collaboration
```

## Public/private contract

The public deck uses only `sparta-public`. Exact receipt paths, mutable corpus counts,
response-route defects, and deployment details remain in the private appendix. The two
variants share one claim ledger so they cannot drift into contradictory stories.

## Non-claims

- F-36 is fictional and synthetic.
- Candidate visibility is not compliance credit.
- Models do not receive approval or signoff authority.
- Prepared-host proof binds to an exact commit.
- The deck does not establish certification, accreditation, customer deployment, or
  endorsement.
