## Position

The README is not a Problem→Solution→Proof arc. Its spine is a **single repeated thesis** — *relevance is not authority; the model navigates, governed evidence and people decide* — restated across five contexts (product view, foundation, demonstration world, mechanism, honest status). The deck's job is to carry that ONE claim through those contexts using the README's own synthetic F-36 captures, and to end on an honest collaboration offer, not a triumphant close. The image inventory is unusually strong: six are real product screenshots (dense-reference / mixed-demo material) and two are conceptual diagrams (assertion+art / divider material). That maps cleanly to the house, but the README's *hedged* register ("in integration," "candidate is not credit," "not a live capture") is the main thing a slide deck can betray. I build to preserve the hedges as visible text, not smooth them away.
