## Arms and contrasts (SEALED)

Four arms, identical approved text, one qualified realization mechanism:
F = flat/static Embry; M = compute-matched direct-memory affect (no dream
synthesis, dream-free derivation prompt); D = certified dream affect;
S = maximally distant, magnitude-matched shuffled dream affect.

- Primary contrast: D vs M (does dreaming add value beyond direct memory?).
- Secondary: D vs S (semantic specificity); D vs F (perceptibility).
- D and M MUST derive from the same frozen residue snapshot (pre-derivation),
  or the contrast confounds dreaming with residue selection.
- Interpretation fixed in advance: D>M and D>S ⇒ dream-specific value.
  D≈M, both >F ⇒ memory-conditioned affect works; dream intermediate not
  demonstrated for voice. D>F but D≈S ⇒ generic actuation. M>D ⇒ dream
  harmful for this endpoint. all≈F ⇒ realization/representation ineffective.

## Pivot rule (SEALED — dissent resolution 1, agent-skills#977)

- Binding kill rule: after the cross-turn pilot, pivot away from the
  dispositional bet ONLY IF the upper 80% context-clustered confidence bound
  for D-over-M preference falls below the minimally-useful rate.
- Minimally-useful rate: initial policy 58% preference; the final number is
  SEAL-AFTER-PILOT (set from the blinded variance-only pilot, before any
  preference ratings are unblinded).
- The simple significance-null point estimate is recorded as an ADVISORY
  early-warning flag; it cannot by itself trigger the pivot.
- Budget guard: the pilot is capped at ONE round of listener spend. Scaling
  beyond the pilot requires the pilot to clear futility AND a new operator
  authorization.

## Preconditions before listener recruitment (SEALED)

1. Gate 0: semantic-equivalence fixture suite exists and passes adversarial
   cases (composer may alter delivery phrasing, never propositional content).
2. Gate 1: one realization mechanism qualified by the 480-render reach screen
   (≥2 preregistered prosodic features clear per-channel flat-repeat P95
   noise; ASR + speaker-cosine noninferiority). Bounded-null is a valid
   terminal result and stops listener work on that mechanism.
3. Gate 2: source-to-affect annotation oracle passes (≥15/20 D profiles get
   2-of-3 blinded support; zero wrong-counterpart passes; annotators never
   see dream prose).
4. This prereg survives one adversarial tau review round.
5. The operator declaration field above is filled or explicitly emptied.
