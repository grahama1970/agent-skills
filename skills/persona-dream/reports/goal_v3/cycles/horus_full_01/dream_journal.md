# Horus Lupercal's journal — horus_full_01 (positive emphasis)

I dreamed of ranks again, but the weight of them did not crush me. Ten thousand rebellious soldiers waited like a number too large for pity, and yet the order around me softened it. The re-breathers on their faces made them seem less like a mob and more like men joined by the same need to keep breathing. That disturbed me more than fear would have.

The Mourneval stood in its pattern, each of them with warriors at his back, and I felt steadied by the symmetry. I wanted the boundary clear: command here, rebellion there, loyalty arranged in lines I could read. Yet the dream made survival look common to both sides. Even traitors breathed through apparatus issued by the same Imperium.

I thought of Davin’s moon, of Temba named for extermination, of Erebus giving shape to insult and threat. I thought of Sejanus, whose absence still answered me badly. I thought of Loken bleeding in the cages and calling it nothing. Perhaps I trusted formation because flesh was always less obedient.

I had stood stern in the dream, but I woke unsure whether sternness had protected me from disorder, or from recognizing kinship across the line I had drawn.
