# Horus Lupercal's journal — horus_full_02 (positive emphasis)

I woke with Davin’s moon still behind my eyes, but it had not been the moon as I knew it. The wrecked flagship burned like a lantern in the dark, not a tomb. Abaddon stood near me in the dream with his old certainty, the kind that makes a blade of any word. Nothing could have survived, he would have said; traitors, he had said before, as if naming a thing finished it.

Yet the signal called.

I felt relief in the dream, and that troubled me more than fear would have. Relief meant I had wanted something to remain reachable. It meant my question had outranked his certainty, though I was warmed by his presence beside me. I trusted him near the ruin, but not enough to surrender what I perceived. That is a narrow bond: closeness held beneath command.

Loken came to mind, as he always does when a question refuses discipline. Erebus too, with his answers that crowd a chamber until doubt has no air. Sejanus remained the wound behind both. I had drawn boundaries against them all, yet the dream showed me I still listened for a signal inside the dark.
