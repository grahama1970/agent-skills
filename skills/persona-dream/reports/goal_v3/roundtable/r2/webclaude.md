All four are trivially fixture-testable, all fail-closed. Q4 — Deterministic prosody receipt. The Galdino review's F0-RMSE/GPE focus fits reference-matched TTS, but our A/B triplet has no ground-truth reference — we are measuring difference from flat baseline, so frame-error metrics apply with the flat rendition as the comparison signal, plus distributional stats. Receipt per cycle, computed deterministically (fixed extractor version pinned in the receipt, e.g. pyworld/praat-parselmouth pinned by hash; audio hashes committed at activation per my round-1 pin): per rendition — F0 mean, F0 SD, F0 range (10th–90th pct), voiced-frame rate, speech rate (syllables/s via forced alignment), mean pause duration, pause count, RMS energy mean/SD. Gates, all relative to same-text flat baseline: (A) reach gate — dream rendition differs from flat by ≥ pre-registered minimum on ≥2 of {F0 SD, speech rate, pause pattern} (weights must reach the waveform, else listener eval is moot); (B) direction gate — the sign of each delta matches the pre-registered per-dream directional hypothesis; (C) noninferiority gate (webgpt) — ASR WER on dream rendition within +1 abs point of flat, speaker-similarity (ECAPA cosine) ≥ 0.90 vs flat, so affect isn't purchased with intelligibility or identity; (D) shuffled-dream rendition must fail (B) — the negative control is part of the receipt, not just the study.
