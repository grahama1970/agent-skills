Q3 — Positioning. Dispositional affect is a real differentiator and the research brief inadvertently proves it: every competitor conditions per-utterance (tags, instructions, semantic inference of this text). None has a persistent, experience-grounded prior that makes the same text sound different because of what the agent has lived. Hume infers emotion from what is said; persona-dream's bet is emotion from what was experienced — orthogonal axes, and the composer architecture (situational hard-overrides + dispositional coloring) is exactly the safe composition of both, which nobody in the brief has. The demonstration that would establish it, and hence GOAL_V5 charter: "Consistent Disposition, Blind-Detectable Across Turns." Design: multi-turn conversations, identical scripts, rendered under persona-A-dream vs persona-B-dream vs no-dream; blinded listeners answer across-turn questions ("which speaker seems more guarded in general?", "did speaker 1's attitude persist?") rather than per-utterance emotion ID. Success = listeners attribute a consistent disposition to the dream-conditioned voice above chance, and the shuffled-persona control fails. Precondition: at least one audible realization channel from Q2 passes its reach gate — V5's step 0 is the (c) probe plus a (b) timing-based reach gate, its step 1 the across-turn listener design, preregistered under protocol-v3 discipline with the direct-memory arm from panel 4 round 2 folded in here as the "memory without dreaming" comparison.
