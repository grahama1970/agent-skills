Hugging Face + 2 B. Research position What is integration. The dream→journal→mood loop is a well-executed instance of the reflection/memory-consolidation pattern from Park et al.'s Generative Agents (2023). The persona-conditioned spoken line (Path B) sits squarely in an active persona-TTS literature: Zhu et al. 2024's P2VA (Persona-to-Voice-Attribute for cross-speaker synthesis), Lee, Kang & Shim's "Voicing Personas" (SKKU, 2025), which rewrites textual persona descriptions into style prompts for controllable TTS with fine-grained prosody manipulation, and most directly ATRIE (2026), which uses LLM chain-of-thought reasoning to infer contextually appropriate emotions from a persona and drives a prosody track over a GPT-SoVITS backbone, with zero-shot generalization to unseen characters. Deterministic ToM-tag→style-string tables are a thin version of what GST (Wang et al., ICML 2018) and prompt-style TTS (PromptTTS, InstructTTS, ParlerTTS) do learned. None of the frozen 5-row table, the temperature formula, or the LLM-writes-the-line move is a contribution. arxiv What is (potentially) genuine. Every system above treats the persona as static — a card, a description, a reference clip. The field's own framing of the gap is maintaining character coherence across long-form generation, i.e., consistency, not change. The Continuity Ledger is the one object here without a clear counterpart: a multi-rate identity model (immutable core with never-resolve conflicts < versioned earned amendments < one additive arc-delta per cycle with a mandatory still_true clause < fast mood), with the invariants mechanically enforced (core-sha assertion on every delta write, amendment blocked if it resolves a conflict).
