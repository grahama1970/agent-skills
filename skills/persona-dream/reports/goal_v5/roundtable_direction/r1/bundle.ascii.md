## What persona-dream IS (grounding -- read first)

The README question, verbatim: **"Can an AI persona dream about what has happened
to it, watch the dream it made, learn from it, and still remain recognizably
itself?"** This is a PERSONA TO BUILD (character-craft + agent architecture), not
a hypothesis to benchmark. The personas (Embry Lawson; Horus Lupercal) are
FICTIONAL characters. There is no safety problem to solve here -- a frozen gate
already guarantees the persona's ANSWERS are content-invariant; instability is
added only through emotion tags and conversation TONE.

## What is BUILT and VERIFIED (this is not speculative)

1. **Multi-persona dreaming, end-to-end.** One profile-driven pipeline works for
   ANY persona, discovered at runtime from the corpus (nothing hardcoded):
   memory residue -> conflict-seeded dream (image-grounded, ArcFace identity gate
   when a face reference exists) -> VLM "watch" -> ToM interpretation -> first-
   person journal -> endogenous, request-INDEPENDENT mood. Proven live for Embry
   (age-band/emotion corpus) AND Horus (book/entity/ToM corpus, different schema):
   two full Horus cycles PASSED (Abaddon + unnamed-soldiers counterparts).
2. **The event-fact / self-narrative FIREWALL.** Two memory layers: episodic
   EVENT-FACT (protected canon) vs the JOURNAL (self-narrative, licensed to
   invent its OWN inner life, structurally barred from inventing history or other
   people's facts; never promoted to event-fact; excluded from re-seeding dreams
   = loop guard). Verified: 0 journal leak into the canon collection.
3. **Cross-persona AFFECT graph (new, executable).** Named entities never overlap
   across fictional worlds (SPARTA vs 40k), but AFFECT does: 12 shared affect
   nodes connect Embry and Horus (emotion:fear/anger, tom:unresolved_thread,
   tom:boundary...). Multi-hop over these connects their emotional states, e.g.
   Embry's chapel hope-without-knowing <-> Horus demanding warp-timing that stays
   unresolved, via tom:unresolved_thread. Today this is a READ-ONLY traversal;
   dreaming keeps counterpart isolation WITHIN one persona.
4. **The Continuity Ledger, now WIRED (just shipped).** A persona-scoped,
   multi-rate self: episodic canon (immutable) < identity_core (rare, versioned,
   READ-MOSTLY) < arc_state (the journal appends ONE additive arc_delta/cycle,
   each carrying a "still_true" that keeps the conflict standing) < mood (fast).
   Embry's identity_core is roundtable-authored; Horus's was BOOTSTRAPPED from his
   own corpus (persistent_conflicts = Horus vs Sejanus/Erebus/Loken). Verified:
   two Horus cycles accumulated two arc_deltas that build on each other, with the
   identity_core asserted unchanged. The journal now READS the ledger (reflects as
   the continuing self; mood arises from carried tensions) and WRITES one delta;
   the next cycle receives the changed arc_state. Loop closed on the journal side.

## The FORK (the decision)

Two directions are now open. We want the next chapter and how they combine.

- **A. SELF-EVOLUTION.** Deepen the single persona: finish the loop (dream
  SELECTOR biases seeding toward current arc_state tensions; mood -> live voice
  across a session; rate-limited identity_core amendments earned over many
  cycles; drift/attractor-collapse instrumentation so the arc keeps producing
  NEW facets, not one renamed attractor).
- **B. CROSS-PERSONA INTERACTION.** Open the world: personas dream ABOUT each
  other (Horus's theory-of-mind of Embry via the shared affect graph), or
  co-appear (there is an existing "void-world" scene: Horus + Embry having tea
  under an umbrella, tyranids in the background, discussing SPARTA Explorer, with
  reference sheets for both). Their conflicted emotional states connect through
  ToM, not shared facts.

## Research brief (Brave, 2026-07-24 -- identical to all seats; a starting point,
## not a limit -- do your own web search and cite)

- **Sophia: A Persistent Agent Framework for Artificial Life** (arXiv 2512.18202)
  -- self-generated learning goals, continuous self-improvement wrapper. Prior art
  for A (self-evolution); position our contribution against it.
- **EvoSpark: Endogenous Interactive Agent Societies for Unified Long-Horizon
  Narrative Evolution** (arXiv 2604.12776) -- MULTI-agent narrative evolution with
  an explicit entity-resolution step to rectify LLM-induced identity
  hallucination. Directly relevant to B (cross-persona) AND identity stability.
- **Layered Mutability: Identity Drift and Governance in Self-Modifying AI
  Agents** (arXiv 2604.14717) -- "for persistent systems the [identity] layer
  becomes an initial condition, not just a safety mechanism." Speaks to our
  READ-MOSTLY identity_core.
- **Generative Agents** (Stanford, 25-agent town) and **TinyTroupe** -- canonical
  multi-agent social simulation with personas; the neighbors for B.
- **Zylos (2026-04)** -- persona built incrementally from narrative summaries,
  "reflecting how real characters develop over story arcs" -- the neighbor for our
  arc_delta approach.
