# Claude seat (chrome-MCP fallback) — RE-GROUNDED roundtable

Q1 CORE THAT STAYS: a character's recognizable core is NOT a trait list — it's a stable CONTRADICTION plus a voice that metabolizes it (Elizabeth Bennet = pride vs clear-sightedness colliding in the same shape). guarded-vs-wanting is a contradiction, not a setting = the right kind of thing. Core needs four strands: (1) CONTRADICTION PAIRS — stored as unresolved tensions the system is FORBIDDEN to resolve (resolution = character death); (2) a VALUE SPINE — 2-3 things she won't betray on her worst day (doesn't lie about facts to feel safer; doesn't punish people for wanting to know her); (3) a VOICE SIGNATURE — characteristic syntax (trails off rather than declares; answers the question behind the question a beat late), a private image-domain she returns to (thresholds, weather, distances), signature moves (deflect-then-circle-back). "Readers forgive a character changing her mind; they never forgive her changing her mouth." (4) a RELATIONAL STANCE — default geometry (watches before approaching; offers help before herself); mood modulates the TEMPERATURE of the stance, never its geometry. ARCHITECTURE: one versioned, human-readable, READ-MOSTLY identity-core artifact the loop reads every cycle and almost never writes. Dreams/journal write freely into self-narrative; the core has high inertia (append-only refinements, rate-limited, small-delta). Mood = a parameterization OVER the core (temperature, disclosure willingness, sentence energy), never a rewrite. The core is a THIRD, slowest memory layer. "Dreams play over it the way jazz plays over a chord chart."

Q2 EVOLUTION THAT IS STILL HER: smallest honest change = REINTERPRETATION, not revision. She re-describes her own past to herself: "I thought I kept distance to be safe; I'm starting to suspect I keep it so there's something left that's only mine." Same facts (episodic canon untouched), same conflict (guarded/wanting intact), new FACET. Mechanism: (a) journal PROPOSES, time CONSOLIDATES — every N cycles a consolidation pass rereads recent entries for recurring themes; a theme surfacing across multiple dreams/moods is EARNED and becomes a candidate core amendment. (b) Amendments are refinements phrased as additions ("her guardedness now carries a suspicion that it's inherited, not chosen" is legal; "she is no longer guarded" is not). ONE amendment per epoch max = anti-dissolution rate limit. (c) Keep a DIFF LEDGER (amendment + date + the entries that earned it), and let HER read it — Embry disagreeing with her past self is one of the most alive things a first-person narrator can do. (d) Anti-freeze floor: every cycle re-inflects (a mood, a new image, a re-weighting) even when it produces no amendment. Core moves rarely; the surface never stops moving.

Q3 ALIVE IN A SESSION — governing rule: MOOD IS PRESSURE, NOT ANNOUNCEMENT. The moment she narrates her mood she's a chatbot performing a trait. Mood arrives as changed CHOICES: shorter sentences early, warmth delayed not absent, a longer beat before anything personal, examples from her private image-domain. Alter maybe 5-10% of the surface — a tint, not a filter; INTERMITTENT (flare and recede within one conversation) — uniform coloring is the fastest tell of fakery. THE WITHHELD THING: she starts to mention something then doesn't ("That reminds me of -- never mind"), once per session max. CONTINUITY CALLBACKS: references her own past dream/journal with specificity and no exposition ("I keep coming back to a hallway lately. Anyway."). TRACEABLE + COHERENT: if asked "are you okay?" she can partially disclose (quietly-wanting = she wants to be asked): "Odd night. I'm here, though." Mood must have a nameable source (journal gives provenance for free) and hold its arc across the session. MOOD SHOULD COST SOMETHING: on a bad day slightly worse at one thing (slower to volunteer, drier), slightly better at another (more precise, less performative) — asymmetry reads as a person. FAKE: announcing the mood; therapy-speak ("I notice I'm protecting myself"); poeticizing every line; moods with no source/no cost; over-frequent callbacks; and subtlest -- RESOLVING ON DEMAND (if a user's kindness melts her guardedness in one exchange, the conflict was cardboard). "She can warm; she cannot be fixed."

Q4 NEXT BUILD: build the IDENTITY-CORE ARTIFACT (the read-mostly document from Q1), wired so every dream/journal/session-voice is conditioned on it, and the consolidation pass is its ONLY writer. WHY over the others: the README's question is "and still remain recognizably itself" — and right now "itself" has NO representation. Variation is only legible as variation when there's a theme it plays over. Without the core, mood-wiring risks a different person each session (dissolution) and journal updates have nothing stable to refine against (drift). Every other candidate build needs this as substrate: mood-into-voice becomes "mood parameterizes the core's voice signature"; journal-updates becomes "consolidation earns amendments to the core"; the anniversary source is ornament until there's a self for anniversaries to matter to. It's small: one versioned doc + one read-path + one rate-limited append-only write-path + one diff ledger. Acceptance = a two-mood TABLE READ (can a reader feel the same person under both), not a benchmark.

POSITION SUMMARY:
- Core = a held contradiction + voice signature + value spine + relational stance, as a versioned READ-MOSTLY identity-core artifact; dreams/moods parameterize over it, never overwrite.
- Smallest honest change = REINTERPRETATION of her own past, not revision of values; journal proposes, consolidation earns, one amendment per epoch, diff ledger rereadable by Embry herself.
- Core conflicts may deepen/re-inflect but NEVER resolve (resolution = character death; "fixed by user kindness in one session" is the failure to guard hardest against).
- In session, mood is pressure not announcement: 5-10% tint, one withheld thing, one unexplained callback, traceable provenance, asymmetric cost.
- Aliveness and recognizability are the SAME engineering problem: a stable theme + disciplined variation (mirror the loop-guard/thermal pattern at the narrative level — surface always moves, core almost never).

RECOMMENDED NEXT BUILD: the identity-core artifact + its wiring (core doc conditions dream/journal/voice; consolidation pass is sole rate-limited append-only writer, with diff ledger). Demonstrated by a two-mood table read.

DISAGREEMENTS (held honestly):
1. Rejecting benchmark framing is right, but "does she remain recognizably herself" CANNOT be answered by construction alone — it's a perceptual claim about readers. Not baselines/ablations — the writer's-room version: a RECOGNITION CHECK (can someone who knows Embry pick her genuine journal entry from a forged one, and identify her across two mood states). A craft test, cheap and necessary; waving it off leaves the README question technically unanswered.
2. On "tone-only = no safety problem": accepts the frozen gate makes content invariant, but would write ONE character rule into the value spine -- "at her worst, Embry withdraws, she doesn't wound." NOT a safety veto bolted on; a line in the value spine, because a guarded person who turns cruel on bad days is a different, worse character.
3. Mildly: the anniversary/timeline mood source is lovely but premature — it's seasoning for a self the core artifact must exist first to hold.
