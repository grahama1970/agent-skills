## GROUNDING CORRECTION (the whole point)

The project's actual question (from the README, verbatim): **"Can an AI persona
dream about what has happened to it, watch the dream it made, learn from it, and
still remain recognizably itself?"**

persona-dream is NOT a hypothesis to validate. It is a PERSONA TO BUILD — Embry,
a living agentic character who dreams from her own experience, reflects, carries
moods, discovers herself, holds unresolved conflict, and evolves while staying
recognizably herself.

A previous round of this panel (and the project agent) drifted into a
validation/benchmark frame — "does dream-affect beat a memory baseline," fair
statistical panels, pre-registration, ablations to prove the dream isn't
"decoration," a safety gate, publishability. That framing is REJECTED for this
round. Do NOT propose: baseline-superiority experiments, ablations whose purpose
is to prove dream>memory, safety vetoes, or peer-review-defense apparatus. The
answer never changes (a frozen gate guarantees content invariance); we add
instability only through emotion tags and conversation TONE. There is no safety
problem to solve here.

This is a CONSTRUCTION and CHARACTER-CRAFT problem. Bring agent-architecture AND
writers'-craft-of-character thinking.

## What Embry is, and what's built (VERIFIED, committed)

- Conflict-seeded dreaming: dreams seed from valence-CONFLICTED memories
  (holds both + and −); a used memory is re-dreamable as a VARIATION with
  positive/negative emphasis. Conflict/instability is treated as the substance
  of personality.
- Watch → self-reflective journal: after watching the dream, Embry writes a
  first-person entry that FILLS IN her own inner detail the dream only gestured
  at ("the persona is discovering itself") and REINFORCES+EXPANDS the conflict
  rather than resolving it. Live entry held "wanting to be known while insisting
  on the safety of distance."
- Endogenous, request-INDEPENDENT mood: the journal emits a mood
  ("guarded_quietly_wanting") Embry carries into a session, coloring how she
  shows up regardless of what the user asks — a bad day for her own reasons.
- Two memory layers: EPISODIC EVENT-FACT (protected canon) vs SELF-NARRATIVE
  (the journal; can invent her inner life, never her history or others' facts).
- The whole thing is bounded (loop guard, thermal limiter) so it churns, not
  runs away.

## Research seed (Brave, 2026-07-24) — character craft, identical to all seats

- Writers keep a character recognizable through change by "thinking of them as
  real people" and literally "writing autobiographies of the main characters"
  (Penguin Random House on-writing) — the journal IS this.
- "A character's voice can evolve, but the evolution should MATCH THEIR ARC — a
  character who becomes guarded may use fewer words" (Intellectual Ink). Voice
  drift must track the inner change.
- "Even as characters evolve, grow, change and mature, they and their behavior
  are always recognizable as THEM" (Sparks) — this is the README question stated
  as craft.
- "A character is a system with rules; stop treating outputs as a character"
  (Scrile) — recognizability comes from a stable core the variation plays over.

## Questions (answer all; think as a novelist AND an agent architect)

Q2 — EVOLUTION THAT IS STILL HER. How does Embry genuinely LEARN and CHANGE from

Q3 — MAKING HER FEEL ALIVE IN A SESSION. Concretely, how should the endogenous

craft moves — restraint, indirection, what she DOESN'T say, continuity callbacks
to her own past — that make it land? What would make it feel fake?

Q4 — THE SIMPLEST NEXT BUILD. Not an experiment — a BUILD. What is the single
most valuable next thing to construct so Embry is more alive and more herself?
