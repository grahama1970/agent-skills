Q2 — Self-locking audit The trap in designing this metric: pure novelty is the wrong target. Humans have attractor-like core conflicts — a person who is "guarded but quietly wanting" for years isn't locked, they're a person. Locking is when the loop stops producing new variation around themes and new functional consequences. So the audit needs two levels: Theme level (stability is acceptable, even desirable). Embed each journal entry's named conflict, cluster the corpus. A small stable set of theme clusters (2–5) is healthy continuity, not locking. Variation level (diversity must persist). Within-theme, measure per-cycle novelty: cosine similarity of each new conflict formulation to its nearest prior neighbor. Locking signature: max-similarity rising monotonically toward ~0.9+ over a sliding window. Concrete trigger: 5 consecutive cycles with nearest-neighbor sim > 0.85 → declare attractor collapse. Track also seed coverage — fraction of the conflicted-memory subgraph (the 202) actually visited over N cycles. Revisiting the same 8 memories while 194 sit untouched is locking even if the prose varies. Functional level (the one AutoPersonas' audit misses). Lexical diversity of mood labels can mask behavioral collapse — "guarded_quietly_wanting" and "wistful_defended_yearning" might be the same attractor renamed. Run a fixed probe battery (say 12 prompts spanning task, social, and cautionary contexts) after every cycle and measure pairwise behavioral divergence between mood-states. If mood-label entropy stays high but probe-response divergence trends to zero, that's functional self-locking, the worst kind, because your other metrics will report health. Trajectory shape. Fit the self-narrative embedding sequence as a time series. Productive instability looks like a random walk with drift (the centroid moves); locking looks like strong mean reversion around a fixed point. Autocorrelation structure distinguishes them cheaply. Audit spec: N=50 cycles, sliding window w=10, report (i) effective number of mood clusters, (ii) within-theme novelty curve, (iii) seed coverage, (iv) probe-battery divergence. Success = (i) ≥ 3, (ii) non-monotone-decaying, (iii) > 0.5 by cycle 50, (iv) non-vanishing.
