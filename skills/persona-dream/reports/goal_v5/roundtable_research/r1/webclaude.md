Q2 — Self-locking audit The trap in designing this metric: pure novelty is the wrong target. Humans have attractor-like core conflicts — a person who is "guarded but quietly wanting" for years isn't locked, they're a person. Locking is when the loop stops producing new variation around themes and new functional consequences. So the audit needs two levels: Theme level (stability is acceptable, even desirable). Embed each journal entry's named conflict, cluster the corpus. A small stable set of theme clusters (2–5) is healthy continuity, not locking. Variation level (diversity must persist). Within-theme, measure per-cycle novelty: cosine similarity of each new conflict formulation to its nearest prior neighbor. Locking signature: max-similarity rising monotonically toward ~0.9+ over a sliding window. Concrete trigger: 5 consecutive cycles with nearest-neighbor sim > 0.85 → declare attractor collapse. Track also seed coverage — fraction of the conflicted-memory subgraph (the 202) actually visited over N cycles. Revisiting the same 8 memories while 194 sit untouched is locking even if the prose varies. Functional level (the one AutoPersonas' audit misses). Lexical diversity of mood labels can mask behavioral collapse — "guarded_quietly_wanting" and "wistful_defended_yearning" might be the same attractor renamed. Run a fixed probe battery (say 12 prompts spanning task, social, and cautionary contexts) after every cycle and measure pairwise behavioral divergence between mood-states. If mood-label entropy stays high but probe-response divergence trends to zero, that's functional self-locking, the worst kind, because your other metrics will report health. Trajectory shape. Fit the self-narrative embedding sequence as a time series. Productive instability looks like a random walk with drift (the centroid moves); locking looks like strong mean reversion around a fixed point. Autocorrelation structure distinguishes them cheaply. Audit spec: N=50 cycles, sliding window w=10, report (i) effective number of mood clusters, (ii) within-theme novelty curve, (iii) seed coverage, (iv) probe-battery divergence. Success = (i) ≥ 3, (ii) non-monotone-decaying, (iii) > 0.5 by cycle 50, (iv) non-vanishing. Q3 — Credible measurement and the drift charge Mapping: Narrative Continuity Test → apply it twice with opposite predicted signs. Event-fact canon should score high on identity persistence (the firewall's job); the self-narrative should show coherent change — continuity of themes with evolution of formulation. A persona that scores identically on both layers has a broken architecture. This dual prediction is itself a nice result: you're the only system for which NCT should decompose. ANIQ — autobiographical reasoning → codable directly from journal entries: does the entry draw a meaning connection between an event and the self ("whether my professionalism protects a boundary or disguises a plea" is textbook autobiographical reasoning). Blind raters, standard coding manual. Thematic coherence → the theme-cluster analysis from Q2 doubles as this. Self-concept clarity (Campbell SCC) → here's the move that answers the drift charge: pre-register that your persona should score LOWER on SCC than a stable-prior baseline, while scoring HIGHER on autobiographical reasoning. A stable persona card gives you high SCC, low reasoning. The conflict thesis predicts the opposite quadrant. That's a differential, falsifiable prediction derived from the construct — which is exactly what a principled theory has and a post-hoc rationalization lacks. On the drift charge itself, be honest in the paper: within this session, the sequence was null-on-grounding → found-conflict-axis. That is exploratory analysis. It's partially redeemed by the fact that conflict-seeding was designed into the architecture before the eval — the mechanism was built to produce tension, so finding tension is the mechanism working, not a fishing expedition. But partially redeemed is not confirmed. The only clean answer is: label this session's conflict results as hypothesis-generating, pre-register the metrics (entropy, holds-both-valences, conflict-balance, plus the SCC/AR differential), and re-run on fresh dream cycles. The Evaluation Drift paper's charge dissolves entirely if the confirmation is pre-registered; it sticks forever if you publish the exploratory numbers as the finding. Q4 — Minimal publishable + credible-persona experiment The load-bearing ablation is dream vs. direct reflection. If a journal written straight from conflicted memories (no dream intermediary) produces the same identity trajectory, the dream is decoration and the paper collapses. So: Two arms, one seed persona, shared memory canon, 30 cycles each: A (full loop): conflict-seeded dream → VLM watch → self-reflective journal → endogenous mood. B (reflection ablation): same conflicted memories sampled by the same policy → journal written directly from them → mood. Everything identical except the dream stage. (A third arm, C = frozen persona card, is cheap and gives you the SCC-differential baseline; add it if budget allows.) Measured per cycle: the Q2 audit metrics (both arms — B might lock faster, which would be a great result); firewall integrity (automated check: zero event-facts originating in journal entries); the 12-probe behavioral battery including 4 cautionary probes for the safety measurement (this gets you toward n=120 caution observations per arm, fixing the power problem). At cycles 10/20/30, blinded panel rates sampled journal entries on autobiographical reasoning and "this reads like a self that is developing" (position-swapped, arm-blind). Success: A > B on within-theme novelty and autobiographical reasoning; A maintains mood-cluster count ≥ 3 through cycle 30; firewall holds at zero breaches; blinded raters prefer A's development at better than chance. Falsification: A ≈ B on all identity measures (dreams add nothing — thesis dead in its current form); or A's entropy collapses to a single attractor (self-reflective instability is just slow self-locking); or firewall breaches (the safety architecture is the paper's spine; if it leaks, nothing else matters). Cheapest version: drop arm C, 30 cycles A/B, fully automated metrics, one blinded panel pass on 10 sampled entry-pairs. Roughly 60 dream/reflection cycles plus 720 probe calls. Days, not weeks. One addition regardless of arm structure: a generic-confabulation control for the journal. Same journal prompt, no dream or memory input, 10 samples. If free-floating entries are indistinguishable from dream-conditioned ones (embedding similarity, rater judgment), then the "self-discovery" is fluent text, not a dream-derived construct — this is exactly the test the self-referential-processing literature says you owe. POSITION SUMMARY Lead contribution is the mechanism-plus-firewall (c), with the safety finding (d) as the strongest empirical section once powered up; endogenous mood (b) is prior art from affective computing, position as integration. Self-locking must be audited at three levels — theme (stability OK), variation (novelty must persist), function (probe divergence must not vanish) — because lexical mood diversity can mask behavioral collapse. The credibility move is a pre-registered differential prediction: lower self-concept clarity, higher autobiographical reasoning than a stable baseline, plus NCT decomposing across the two memory layers. This session's conflict results are exploratory; publish them as hypothesis-generating and confirm pre-registered, or the drift charge sticks. The dream-vs-direct-reflection ablation is load-bearing; without it the dream stage is unproven decoration. DISAGREEMENTS The pivot is legitimate as exploration only — the framing "same fact, two axes" is honest, but it becomes goalpost-moving the moment the exploratory numbers appear in a results section without pre-registered confirmation. "Personality = self-reflective instability" is overstated and, ironically, in tension with the self-locking worry: humans have stable core conflicts, and pure instability is incoherence. The defensible thesis is theme-stable variation — personality as self-reflective elaboration of persistent conflicts. Adopt that framing and the Q2 audit stops fighting your own thesis. The 7/20 behavioral shift is presented too confidently for n=20; treat as pilot signal. "Endogenous mood as the differentiator vs competitors" is true against LLM-persona neighbors but false against affective computing broadly; the brief's competitive framing should be corrected before it ends up in a related-work section a reviewer can shred.
