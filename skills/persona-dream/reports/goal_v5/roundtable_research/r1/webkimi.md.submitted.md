## What persona-dream now is (all VERIFIED, committed at agent-skills@main)

The thesis crystallized this session: **personality = self-reflective
instability.** Not a stable trait prior — a self that keeps turning its own
unresolved conflicts over and is changed by doing so.

The loop:
1. **Conflict-seeded dreaming.** Dreams seed from valence-CONFLICTED memories
   (202/312 hold both + and − emotion), graph-traversed within a counterpart;
   a used memory is re-dreamable as a VARIATION with positive/negative emphasis
   (instability made mechanical). Counterpart-isolation + loop-guard preserved.
2. **Watch → self-reflective journal.** After a VLM "watches" the dream, Embry
   writes a first-person entry that FILLS IN her own inner detail the dream only
   gestured at ("the persona is discovering itself") and REINFORCES+EXPANDS the
   conflict rather than resolving it. Live proof (conflicted Kai cycle): a
   190-word entry holding "wanting to be known while insisting on the safety of
   distance," expanded understanding = "whether my professionalism protects a
   boundary or disguises a plea to be understood without speaking."
3. **Endogenous, request-INDEPENDENT mood.** The journal emits a session mood
   ("guarded_quietly_wanting") that Embry carries into a user session and that
   colors HOW she shows up regardless of what the user asks. This is the
   differentiator vs reactive/exogenous emotion (competitors derive emotion
   from the user's message; Embry has a bad day for her own reasons).
4. **Two memory layers, like a human.** EPISODIC EVENT-FACT (protected canon)
   vs SELF-NARRATIVE (the journal; memory_kind=self_narrative,
   never_promote_to_event_fact, loop-guard-excluded from seeding). Self-
   discovery is confabulation of self-story, NOT of history or others' facts.

## Fresh research brief (Brave, 2026-07-24, identical for all seats)

Direct neighbors / competitors:
- **AutoPersonas: Multi-Timescale Loop Engine for Open-Ended Persona Evolution**
  (arXiv 2607.08252) — a recursively self-evolving persona loop that NAMES the
  key failure mode: "SELF-LOCKING = the loop keeps generating events/reflections
  but functional diversity collapses into a narrow attractor of old state /
  relationships." They ship an AUDIT for it. Our self-reflective instability is
  the deliberate opposite of self-locking — but we must PROVE we avoid it.
- Evolving Agents (2404.02718), Structured Personality Control (2601.10025),
  Fluid Personality Framework (2607.01034), Consistently Simulating Human
  Personas w/ multi-turn RL (which concedes consistency is "narrow and static";
  real identity is dynamic).
Evaluation instruments we could adopt for credibility:
- **The Narrative Continuity Test** (arXiv 2510.24831) — a framework for
  evaluating identity persistence in AI systems.
- Psychology: **ANIQ** (Awareness of Narrative Identity Questionnaire) and
  **NISE** (Narrative Identity Self-Evaluation) — validated constructs:
  autobiographical reasoning, thematic coherence, self-concept clarity.
Cautions:
- **Evaluation Drift in LLM Personality Induction** (2605.16996) — warns about
  moving the goalpost (relevant: I moved from grounding to conflict THIS
  session; is that principled or post-hoc?).
- **LLMs Report Subjective Experience Under Self-Referential Processing** —
  self-reports "distinguishable from generic confabulation"; bears on whether
  the journal's self-discovery is a measurable construct or just fluent text.
