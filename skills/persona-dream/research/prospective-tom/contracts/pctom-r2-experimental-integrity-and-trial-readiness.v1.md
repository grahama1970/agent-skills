This contract freezes the next Persona Dream research objective after the
bounded PCTOM-R mechanism lane. It is a text-first research-readiness contract,
not a media, provider, dashboard, or subjective dream-quality contract.


## Scope

In scope:

- scope-indexed evidence custody and automatic receipt discovery;
- archive manifest integrity and re-read hashing;
- audit mutation testing against tampered local evidence;
- accepted-source lineage from observations into beliefs, dream branches,
  commitments, scores, and revisions;
- pre-seal outcome isolation with reveal canaries;
- deterministic probabilistic scoring and explicit abstention accounting;
- fault-oracle outcome classes for memory, model, tool, audit, and persistence
  boundaries;
- crash-safe restart and idempotent transition checks;
- deterministic corpora for Memory, compute-matched Memory, free-CD,
  structured-CD, and functional-ToM pilots;
- a sealed, endpoint-aligned powered-trial protocol for future live work.

Out of scope:

- executing the powered live efficacy study;
- claiming that counterfactual dreaming improves prediction or planning;
- paid provider calls, video generation, voice generation, or media-provider
  readiness;
- subjective dream prose, dream-aesthetic, or narrative-quality scoring;
- human or LLM content judgment;
- dashboards or polished human-facing status surfaces as proof;
- canonical memory, identity, or source-memory mutation.
