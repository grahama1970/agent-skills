## Research-Backed Vocal Biomarkers

Voice design is informed by research on trauma, suppression, and cultural emotional norms.

### Suppression Detection (From Dogpile Research)

The voice cannot lie for long. Even deliberate suppression produces measurable leakage:

| Marker | Normal | Suppression |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **F0 Variability** | ~0.15 | >0.25 (autonomic dysregulation) |
| **Jitter** | <1% | 2.5-3.5% (laryngeal tension) |
| **Pitch Breaks** | None | 50-150ms during flat delivery |
| **Pauses** | 200-600ms | >1000ms before difficult content |

### Defense Mechanisms in Voice

Different psychological defense mechanisms produce distinct acoustic signatures:

| Defense | Acoustic Pattern | TTS Guidance |
|---------|------------------|--------------|
| **Repression** | Monotonic, F0 range <30 Hz | Minimal pitch variation, extended pauses |
| **Denial** | Incongruent cheerfulness, rapid speech | Artificially bright energy, rushed pacing |
| **Intellectualization** | "Lecture voice" - steady, controlled | Low pitch variation, measured pace |
| **Dissociation** | Flat, breathy, hollow quality | Hollow breathiness, detached energy |

### Grief Processing Trajectory

| State | F0 | Jitter | HNR | Pauses |
|-------|----|----|-----|--------|
| **Unprocessed** | +15-40 Hz elevated | >2.5% | <15 dB | >1000ms (avoidant) |
| **Processed** | Normalized | <1.5% | >20 dB | 200-600ms (reflective) |

Resilient grievers normalize within 6-12 months; prolonged grief maintains acute markers.

### Cultural Emotional Norms

Different cultures have distinct emotional display rules that affect voice:

| Culture | Display Rules | Suppression Level | Voice Impact |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Greco-Roman Stoic** | Philosophical detachment | 0.7 | Controlled, deliberate, rhetorical |
| **Victorian English** | "Stiff upper lip" | 0.9 | Restrained, minimal variation |
| **Buddhist Equanimity** | Non-attachment | 0.5 | Centered, gentle, mindful pauses |
| **Mediterranean Expressive** | Open emotion valued | 0.2 | Dynamic, wide pitch range |
| **East Asian Collectivist** | Restraint for harmony | 0.75 | Modulated, subtle, considered |

### Family Structure Effects

Family dynamics shape attachment and emotional expression:

| Factor | Voice Impact |
|--------|--------------|
| **Orphaned early** | Self-reliant, guarded attachment |
| **Eldest child** | Responsibility, leadership weight |
| **Only child** | Self-sufficient, articulate |
| **Unstable family** | Adaptable, trust caution |

### Religious Context

Religion provides emotional scaffolding that affects voice:

| Tradition | Voice Characteristics |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Devout Christian** | Conviction, hope, moral weight |
| **Buddhist/Eastern** | Equanimity, non-attachment |
| **Stoic Philosophy** | Self-mastery, acceptance |
| **Jewish Tradition** | Questioning, scholarly probing |

### Interview: New Questions (v2)

The voice design interview now includes:

10. **Family Structure**
    - "What was their family situation?"
    - Birth order, family stability, early loss

11. **Religion/Spirituality**
    - "What was their religious context?"
    - Tradition, denomination, level of devotion

12. **Cultural Emotional Norms**
    - "What norms governed emotional expression?"
    - Maps to cultural suppression databases

13. **Current Age**
    - "At what age should we portray them?"
    - Affects voice quality (elder vs prime vs young)

14. **Recent Impactful Event**
    - "What recent event affects their current state?"
    - Adds emotional "freshness" to current layer

15. **Suppression Level**
    - "How much do they suppress emotions?"
    - Generates appropriate microstructural leakage
