# Battle arena audio attribution

## Score package v1 (provisional GM renders)

Original Battle score package (`battle.music_package.v1`).
All melodies, rhythms, harmonies and arrangements are newly composed for Battle.

Browser delivery assets under `score/v1/*.ogg` and `score/v1/motifs/*.ogg` are
**provisional production assets**: General MIDI / TimGM6mb SoundFont renders.
They prove composition, timing, harmony, and receipt-backed event bindings.
Custom synth patches, stems, and final mix will replace these files without
changing event bindings or URLs under `score/v1/` (or via catalog swap).

Editable MIDI sources: `score/midi/`.
Manifest + style guide + proofs: `score/source/`.

License: original Battle composition (package originality statement).
