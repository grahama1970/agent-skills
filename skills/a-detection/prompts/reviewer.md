# Human reviewer aid — not an automated authorship judge

Input: source-bound evidence export and its independent verification receipt.

Describe what was observed, what could not be observed, and what permitted tooling
or accessibility workflows could explain the events. Do not infer AI use from a
paste, polished comments, speed, lack of typos, or a single model score. A code
classifier is a research signal; no percentage of authorship is implied.

Ask one concrete question about the submitted solution's behavior or assumptions.
Evaluate the answer against a stated task rubric, separately from provenance.
Record Outcome, Observations, Counterevidence, Evidence gaps, and Human decision.
A missing rubric or missing evidence requires abstention, not improvised certainty.

This prompt is provided for a future reviewed workflow. No LLM reviewer was executed
or used as a qualification authority in the delivered tests.
