# Dream Assessment

## Scene 1
**Visual:** A vast, dimly lit library stretches endlessly, shelves warping into impossible geometries. Books float mid-air, their pages fluttering like restless wings. A figure with shoulder-length brown hair, features sharp but unglamorous, stands at the center, pulling a book from the void. The air shimmers with a faint, coppery haze.
**Narration:** The spines of the books whisper in a language I can’t understand. I reach for one, and the ceramite cold of its cover bites my fingertips.

## Scene 2
**Visual:** A scaffold of glowing, translucent threads weaves through a starless void. Monkeys swing silently across the threads, their movements precise, almost ritualistic. The threads hum with energy, and the air smells faintly of ozone and old books.
**Narration:** The scaffold is a map, a lattice of questions I can’t answer. The monkeys move like clockwork, their eyes holding secrets I’ll never grasp.

## Scene 3
**Visual:** A docking interface materializes in the void, its 12 active hooks glowing faintly blue. The air is thick with the metallic tang of iron, and the hooks hum with a low, resonant frequency. The dream-body feels heavy, as if gravity has doubled.
**Narration:** The hooks beckon, but my limbs are leaden. The taste of iron coats my tongue, sharp and unyielding.

## Scene 4
**Visual:** A figure with sharp features and messy brown hair stands on a cliff overlooking a swirling, kaleidoscopic ocean. The air smells of salt and something sweet, like overripe fruit. The figure’s hands grip the edge, fingers trembling with tension.
**Narration:** The ocean churns with questions I can’t answer. The sweetness in the air is cloying, almost suffocating.

## Scene 5
**Visual:** A cluster of glowing orbs floats in a dark void, each orb pulsing with a different rhythm. The air is thick with the scent of lho-stick smoke, and the dream-body feels stretched, as if reaching for something just out of grasp.
**Narration:** The orbs are answers, but they slip through my fingers. The smoke curls around me, heavy and intoxicating.

## Scene 6
**Visual:** A vast, empty theater flickers into view, the screen showing static. The air smells of burnt sugar, and the dream-body feels weightless, floating just above the seats. The screen suddenly flashes with the word 'Contact' in bold, glowing letters.
**Narration:** The static hums with a promise I can’t decipher. The sweetness in the air turns bitter, like ash on my tongue.
