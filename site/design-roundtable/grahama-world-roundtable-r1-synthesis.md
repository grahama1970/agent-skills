## Position

Adopt a provisional **Proof Workshop / Annotated Evidence Ledger** world.

The site should feel like an inspectable proof environment: a claim enters unresolved, evidence becomes the dominant object, gaps remain visible, and judgment is visibly bounded by the artifact record. The world should be recognized from this causal syntax, not from palette, logo, generic dark-editorial polish, or receipt-paper styling alone.

## Dissent Preserved

The main split is between **ledger as architecture** and **proof assembly as architecture**.

Use the synthesis this way:

- Accept the ledger/receipt as a core component primitive.
- Do not let every page become a stack of identical receipt cards.
- Treat receipt-paper material as artifact material, not global page skin.
- Let page composition vary, but force every variation to preserve claim, evidence, gap, and bounded-judgment semantics.

## Three Territories For Boards

### T1 Proof Workshop

Premise: The site is an active proof assembly. Claims, evidence, traces, gaps, and bounded judgments are arranged as semantic objects.

Why it belongs: It directly translates the existing premise into structure and supports artifact-first receipts without requiring heavy runtime effects.

Risks: Fake industrial marks, connector ornament, or too much technical decoration. Must avoid SPARTA-like evidence panels and generic node diagrams.

### T2 Annotated Evidence Ledger

Premise: The site is a typeset ledger of auditable claims with a persistent evidence rail and explicit boundary labels.

Why it belongs: It uses receipt infrastructure and deterministic metadata while creating a recognizable non-color silhouette.

Risks: Bureaucratic coldness, repeated cards, tax-form stiffness, or making the receipt container the whole brand.

### T3 Calibration Path

Premise: The About and method flows become measured paths where waypoints carry evidence payloads and unresolved gaps remain visible.

Why it belongs: It gives the "about path-first" candidate a real proof function instead of a decorative career timeline.

Risks: Decorative map behavior, generic provenance graph, convergence with other Graham-owned systems, or path logic that fails on mobile.

## Recommended Direction

Select **Proof Workshop** as the primary world and borrow the ledger's disciplined evidence rail and the path's causal sequencing only where they do real work.

This avoids two failure modes:

- a generic receipt-card template where labels are renamed but layout stays swappable;
- a graph/map world that becomes expensive, decorative, and similar to SPARTA or persona-dream surfaces.

## Protected Invariants

1. Role geometry: claims are open, evidence is artifact-shaped and locator-bearing, gaps are visibly discontinuous, and judgments are bounded.
2. Evidence dominance: the primary artifact carries more visual weight than commentary or conclusion.
3. Typeface separation: human narrative and machine-emitted values do not share a typeface; monospace remains machine-output-only.
4. Mandatory boundary: every judgment has an explicit local scope/boundary treatment adjacent to the judgment it constrains.
5. Endpoint integrity: every line, rail, trace, or connector resolves between identifiable semantic objects; decorative rules are removed.
6. Static equivalence: motion may reveal sequence but cannot carry essential meaning.
7. Compositional variation: sections share causal grammar but do not repeat the same label-heading-card entrance.

## Genericity And Leakage Risks

- Competitor swap risk remains untested; a generic AI consultancy could still inherit a claim/evidence/judgment card stack.
- Dark ground, cream cards, mono metadata, and warm accents risk converging with other Graham-owned sites if the structure is not distinctive.
- Decorative section serials and ornamental rules are still a template-residue risk unless replaced with functional provenance hierarchy.
- A competence matrix is useful as an evidence index, but matrix-first as the homepage risks becoming a resume taxonomy.
- Material cosplay is forbidden: no fake stamps, screws, tape, lab marks, binder tabs, graph grids, or paper grain unless they encode a real evidence state.

## Impeccable Comparison Model

Impeccable should compare future implementation against this frozen direction, not generic "premium" polish:

- Type fidelity: declared roles for narrative, claim, evidence locator, machine output, judgment, qualifier, and navigation; verify computed family, weight, width, line length, wrapping, and zero mono on human labels.
- Material fidelity: every primitive must map to an evidence state: artifact edge, provenance locator, trace, gap, amendment, and judgment boundary. Treatments without a state are decoration.
- Amend-loop integrity: every finish fix must cite the direction contract, produce before/after screenshots, run deterministic checks, and avoid silent content or global token changes.
- Precedence: open type/material contradictions outrank polish nits.
