# grahama.co — design contract

This file is the human-readable design intent for day-to-day site work. It sits
beside:

- `site/BRAND.md` — brand/personality/audience intent;
- `site/VOICE.md` — prose and first-person voice contract;
- `site/design-world.yml` — machine-readable visual-world contract;
- `site/DESIGN_WORLD.md` — readable visual-world summary.

## Design goal

Make grahama.co feel like Graham's artifact room: dark, curious, severe,
entertaining, technically exact, and personal. It should be discovered rather
than consumed as a normal value proposition.

Do not redesign it into a conventional R&D-tech, AI consultancy, or defense-tech
portfolio.

## Core principles

1. **Discovery over pitch** — the homepage may withhold full comprehension. It
   should offer trails, not spoon-feed a sales deck.
2. **Weirdness with receipts** — unusual juxtapositions are good; unsupported
   claims are not.
3. **Bespoke before category** — components should feel authored for this body
   of work, not transferable to a competitor.
4. **Human narrative vs machine evidence** — preserve the typographic and spatial
   split from `site/design-world.yml`.
5. **Project-specific worlds** — Tau, Sparta, Memory, Extractor, voice/dream, and
   creative systems may share a shell, but should not collapse into one generic
   visual language.
6. **Accessibility is not optional** — curiosity filters are about taste, not
   broken controls, missing names, unreadable text, or motion traps.

## Acceptable friction

Allowed:

- mystery;
- density;
- odd labels;
- artifact trails;
- nonlinear exploration;
- technical receipts and proof boundaries;
- creative/defense/agent-systems collision.

Not allowed:

- wrong links;
- stale counts;
- fake proof;
- inaccessible controls;
- hidden source boundaries;
- corporate jargon pretending to be clarity.

## Visual review rule

Any browser-visible visual judgment must start from a current Surf capture of
the actual page or tab when Surf is available. Source files, cached screenshots,
DOM text, or headless browser output are secondary diagnostics only.

A valid review cites the screenshot path and states the proof boundary.

## Change policy

Prefer targeted slices:

- factual/provenance repair;
- accessibility/name repair;
- one project visual becoming more project-specific;
- one discovery path becoming more rewarding;
- one proof boundary becoming more exact.

Avoid broad redesigns unless the human explicitly asks for one. The goal is not
"make it make sense." The goal is: make the weirdness intentional and the facts
exact.

## Rejection tests

Reject or revise any proposed change that:

- makes the site look more like Straive, Palantir, or generic R&D-tech;
- replaces Graham's first-person voice with company voice;
- demotes the creative/strange side into decoration;
- treats lack of immediate comprehension as a defect by itself;
- uses proof language without proof;
- claims a design is ready without current visual evidence.
