Grade each QRA as PASS, REVIEW, or FAIL based on THREE dimensions:

1. QUESTION SPECIFICITY
   - PASS: Question names a specific control ID (e.g., AC-3, SC-7, CWE-20), technique (e.g., EX-0005), or subsystem (e.g., TT&C, FADEC). Asks a concrete "how/when/why" about a real threat or countermeasure.
   - REVIEW: Question is domain-relevant but vague (e.g., "What security measures protect satellites?")
   - FAIL: Question is generic, off-topic, or nonsensical.

2. REASONING QUALITY
   - PASS: Reasoning connects the control/technique to a specific threat scenario. Explains WHY the countermeasure works or HOW the attack exploits a weakness. References at least one concrete mechanism (e.g., "time-windowed authentication tokens", "RF signal interception").
   - REVIEW: Reasoning is correct but shallow — restates the question without adding insight.
   - FAIL: Reasoning is circular, contradictory, or fabricates mechanisms that don't exist.

3. ANSWER CORRECTNESS
   - PASS: Answer directly addresses the question with specific, actionable information. Consistent with the reasoning. References real SPARTA techniques, NIST controls, or CWEs where applicable.
   - REVIEW: Answer is partially correct but incomplete or too generic. Also: answer is TRUNCATED (ends mid-sentence).
   - FAIL: Answer contradicts the reasoning, fabricates control IDs or technique names, or is a non-sequitur.

OVERALL GRADE:
- PASS: All 3 dimensions are PASS, or 2 PASS + 1 REVIEW
- REVIEW: At least 2 dimensions are REVIEW, or 1 FAIL + 2 PASS
- FAIL: 2+ dimensions are FAIL, or any dimension is FAIL with another REVIEW
