# Whisperheads — Song Brief

## Concept
A dark, atmospheric track about tuning into frequencies others can't hear. The "whisperheads" are those who listen to the static between stations — the noise left behind by absence.

## Mood & Emotional Arc
- **Verse**: Quiet dread, intimate whisper building to spoken word. Fear → sadness.
- **Chorus**: Defiant declaration. The whisperheads claim their identity. Trust → controlled anger.
- **Bridge** (future): Breakdown into pure noise, then silence. Joy of release.

## Musical Influences
- Radiohead — "Kid A" era texture and unease
- Portishead — trip-hop darkness, Beth Gibbons vocal vulnerability
- Nine Inch Nails — "The Fragile" dynamics (quiet→loud)

## Lore Connections
- Horus's recurring theme of signal vs noise
- Dream residue: "frequencies beneath frequencies" (journal 2026-02-28)
- Character arc: learning to hear what others filter out

## Target Sound
- BPM: 85 (slow, deliberate)
- Key: D minor (classic darkness)
- Instrumentation: sparse keys, deep bass, atmospheric pads, drums enter at chorus
- Vocal: starts whispered, builds to full belt at chorus hook
- Production: lo-fi crackle in verse, clean/wide in chorus
